Version: 0.1.1
Date: 2026-10-17
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed a crash when opening the customization window.
    - A look change that has to wait no longer empties the player's hand; it waits until the hand is empty.
    - The window no longer overwrites the hex/RGB value being typed while a look change is waiting.
    - The window now closes when the player dies or rejoins instead of staying open without Esc closing it.
    - Turning off the top-left button no longer leaves an empty box behind.
    - Shows the right status message during cutscenes.
    - Look changes no longer apply in the same tick handcrafting finishes or is cancelled.

Date: 2026-10-16
